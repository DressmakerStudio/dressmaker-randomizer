Dressmaker Random Challenge Generator

Let Fate Design Your Next Dress!

1. Dress construction

Choose the rolled category in game. Count available patterns left to right, top to bottom, and roll a number.

Optional compatible pieces

Leave sleeves or collar at 0 to skip. Changing bodice resets their counts.

2. Fabric tags

No duplicates, Warm + Cool, or Casual + Formal combinations.

Required fabric type
Cool excludes Wool. The type can also satisfy a fabric tag.

3. Color palette

50% monochrome; otherwise 2–3 unique colors.

4. Accessory challenge

25% chance of a required accessory. Quantities are custom challenge rules.

5. Your design brief

Created by a Dressmaker player for fellow Dressmaker players. The challenge concept, gameplay rules, and creative decisions are creator-designed, with AI assistance used to develop and troubleshoot the interactive generator.

This is a free, unofficial fan-made project and is not affiliated with the developers of Dressmaker.
